Audience interest summary

Who is searching, and what that means for VoltEdge.

Who
Students aged 18 to 24, searching mostly on their phones.
Interests
Mobile phones and gadgets, online shopping, music and gaming, study and productivity setups.
Intent
Searches like "price in pakistan" show people comparing prices and ready to buy, so clear PKR prices matter.
Peak times
Start of each semester, Eid, and the 11.11 and Black Friday sales.

Takeaway: start Google Ads on low-competition keywords like "laptop stand" for cheaper clicks, and promote STUDENT10 on the high-volume earbuds and phone cover searches.

How the data is collected

Steps to fill the table with real results.

  1. Open Google Ads, then Tools, then Keyword Planner.
  2. Choose Get search volume and forecasts and paste the keywords.
  3. Set location to Pakistan and currency to PKR.
  4. Copy searches, competition and top-of-page bids into js/keywords.js.
