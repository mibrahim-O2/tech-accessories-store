01 · Marketing strategy

Reach students where they scroll

Three channels that work together: ads bring people in, videos build trust, and the student discount turns them into buyers.

Meta Ads

Facebook and Instagram ads aimed at students aged 18 to 25 in major Pakistani cities.

  • Interest targeting: gadgets, mobile phones, university life
  • Carousel ads using our white-background product photos
  • Retarget people who viewed the catalog but did not order

TikTok unboxing videos

Short, honest videos that show the product out of the box and in real use.

  • 15 to 30 second unboxings of each product
  • "Charge test" and "desk setup" videos that show results
  • Work with student creators on campus

Student discounts

10% off every order with a valid student ID, using the code STUDENT10.

  • Shown on the home page and in every ad
  • Works together with free delivery and cash on delivery
  • Pushed hardest at the start of each semester
02 · Imaging plan

Two kinds of photos for every product

Clean studio shots to show exactly what you get, and desk lifestyle shots to show how it fits into student life.

White-background product shots

Done
  • Square 1:1 images on a pure white background
  • Used on the catalog, product cards and carousel ads
  • Soft, even lighting so colours look true

Desk lifestyle shots

Planned
  1. Study desk: earbuds beside a notebook and coffee, laptop open in the background.
  2. Hostel setup: laptop raised on the aluminum stand with an external keyboard.
  3. Bedside charge: 20W charger plugged in, phone charging on a side table.
  4. On campus: phone in the navy cover held in hand, university building behind.
  • Natural window light with warm tones that match the brand
  • Used for Instagram posts and TikTok thumbnails

05 · Area of success

Why this business can work

A clear customer group and products that people buy again and again.

University students

Our main customers use their phones and laptops all day for classes, assignments and entertainment, but have tight budgets.

  • Prices from Rs. 800 fit a student budget
  • Very active on Instagram and TikTok, where we advertise
  • Word of mouth spreads fast in hostels and classes

High repeat purchases

Accessories wear out, get lost or need replacing when a phone is upgraded, so happy customers come back.

  • Covers and cables are replaced often
  • A first cheap buy (cover) builds trust for a bigger one (earbuds)
  • 1-year warranty and cash on delivery keep trust high
